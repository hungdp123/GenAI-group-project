## Read this before quoting any number

- **This is a pipeline check, not a result.** The corpus is one fictional company, FinSight's agents
  are stubs, and the dev gold labels were written alongside the stub corpus. So FinSight's 14/14 is
  circular (see `docs/decisions.md`, Known limitations).
- **The baseline numbers are real `gpt-5.4` calls.** Total cost for this run was about $0.07.
- **Qualitative findings worth keeping for the case studies:**
  - The direct baseline computed "about 14%" revenue growth itself from the pasted XBRL numbers. FinSight forbids LLM-generated numbers.
  - The direct baseline merged "revenue growth" and "biggest winner" into one claim and labelled it CONTRADICTED. FinSight splits them.
  - With the same refusal and scope policy in its prompt, `gpt-5.4` refused every advice and out-of-scope item. Refusal alone does not separate the systems.
- **Latency is not comparable:** the baselines call the API, while FinSight ran local stubs.

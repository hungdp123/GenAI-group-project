    How to check a claim

    Write what the influencer said, the way you would tell a friend:

    Who said which company did what, with a number

    1. Name the influencer: Joseph Carlson, Mark Roussin, Alex Reffett, or Kevin Paffrath. You can also pick one from the Influencer list.
    2. Include a figure FinSight can look up: revenue, growth, margin, profit, or cash flow.
    3. Optionally set Check as of to look only at videos and filings up to that day.
    4. Press Run check. You get a verdict with its source. Details holds the evidence, Steps, and the full trace.

    FinSight will not check

    • Advice: "Should I buy Amazon?"
    • Price predictions: "Nvidia stock will hit $500 next year."
    • Whole-market claims: "The S&P 500 is up 13% this year."
    • Claims nobody made: a statement with no influencer gets a follow-up question first.
